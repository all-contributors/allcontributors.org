## Add New Contributors in Seconds

Thanking everyone who helps your project shouldn't mean editing your `README.md`
by hand. The All Contributors app and CLI add contributors to your table for
you, so you can focus on your project.

## How the app works

<div style={{ display: "flex", flexWrap: "wrap", alignItems: "center", gap: "2rem" }}>

  <div style={{ flex: "1", minWidth: "300px" }}>
    <Steps>
      1. **Install the app on your repository**<br />
         The installation guide shows how
      2. **Open an issue or pull request, or use an existing one**
      3. **Mention `@all-contributors`**
      4. **Add a username and a contribution type**<br />
         The emoji key lists the contribution types
      5. **Merge the pull request the app opens**<br />
         The first one also adds the contributors table
    </Steps>
  </div>
